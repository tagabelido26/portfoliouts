UNDERSTANDING THE SELF
BSN 1A
Personal Development Portfolio — Understanding the Self

A glimpse of who I am, seen through four lenses.

Every self is over-described and under-known. This is a portfolio for Understanding The Self — four perspectives (philosophy, sociology, anthropology, psychology) turned towards one person: Me

PHILOSOPHICAL PERSPECTIVE

Who am I beyond what I do?

I am more than the roles I play in life. This perspective invites me to reflect on my beliefs, values, purpose, and the experiences that have shaped my identity. The photographs I selected capture moments that influenced the person I am today and the person I continue to strive to become.

"I act, therefore I exist" - Gilbert Ryle.

I'm always thankful for everything that has happened to me. My time at BCNSHS helped me understand myself more, eventually finding my strengths, values, and purpose. Thanks to that, I have slowly figured out my identity such as the things that I value, the things that I do which are meaningful to me, and the man I eventually want to become.

SOCIOLOGICAL PERSPECTIVE

How have the people around me shaped who I am?

My relationships and the communities I belong to have influenced my identity in many ways. Through my friends, classmates, and organizations, I have learned responsibility, leadership, teamwork, and compassion. These photographs reflect the people who continue to shape the person I am becoming.

I've always wanted everyone to be a part of something, I don't like seeing others get left out. This has molded me to enhance my leadership and teamwork skills, as I have experienced being lonely and don't want others to feel the same way I did. Eventually, talking to people and making connections became meaningful, and easier, not only having an impact on them, but mine as well. This is why I treasure my friends, and people around me and treat them as family, because they mean so much to me, and how I became me.

ANTHROPOLOGICAL PERSPECTIVE

How has my culture shaped who I am?

My culture, traditions, and heritage are important parts of who I am. This perspective allows me to reflect on the customs, beliefs, celebrations, and experiences that connect me to my family, community, and Filipino identity.

A big part of who I am today is because of the culture and traditions that shaped me. There are a lot of beliefs, practices, and customs passed down to me that I still do. Thanks to my family, I've become self aware in what I should keep, and stop doing in traditions. An example of one that I am thankful for is making me have strong faith, as it has always helped me in my journey.

PSYCHOLOGICAL PERSPECTIVE

What do my thoughts and emotions reveal about me?

Looking inward helps me better understand my personality, emotions, strengths, weaknesses, and personal growth. The photographs I selected represent experiences that reveal how I think, feel, overcome challenges, and continue to grow into a better version of myself.

These photos explain the mindset I have today, according to Carl Rogers' Self Concept theory. Going to the gym to improve myself, my goofy and fun character when I was a child that I still have, the sense of accomplishment in doing good, the joy in helping, and the hunger to be better. These pictures tell the story of my self-image, self worth, and ideal-self.
